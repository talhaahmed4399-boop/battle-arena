BATTLE ARENA ESPORTS FEDERATION

RULES BOOK

Competitive standards for fair, professional and respectful Free Fire esports.

100 RULES
01 CODE
FF ESPORTS
IMPORTANT

These are Battle Arena Esports Federation competition rules. They are not a reproduction of Garena's official rulebook. Tournament-specific announcements may add or modify requirements when clearly published before competition.

01

GENERAL CONDUCT

01.

Every participant must follow the Battle Arena rulebook and the specific rules published for the tournament.

02.

Players must provide accurate registration information.

03.

Players must use their registered in-game identity during competition.

04.

Participants must respect opponents, teammates, administrators and tournament staff.

05.

Abusive, threatening or discriminatory behaviour is prohibited.

06.

Players must follow instructions issued by authorized tournament staff.

07.

Attempts to manipulate tournament administration are prohibited.

08.

Players must not intentionally interfere with another team's participation.

09.

Participants must maintain professional conduct before, during and after matches.

10.

Ignorance of a published rule does not automatically excuse a violation.

02

PLAYER ELIGIBILITY

11.

Every player must satisfy the tournament's published eligibility requirements.

12.

A player may represent only the team registered for that competition.

13.

Players must not use another person's account or identity.

14.

Account credentials must never be shared for competitive play.

15.

Players are responsible for the security of their own accounts.

16.

Suspended players may not participate until their suspension is officially cleared.

17.

Players must notify tournament staff about eligibility problems before a match whenever possible.

18.

False eligibility information may result in disqualification.

19.

Organizers may request reasonable verification where tournament rules permit it.

20.

Eligibility decisions must be based on the published tournament requirements.

03

TEAM MANAGEMENT

21.

Every participating team must have an officially registered roster.

22.

A team captain is responsible for communicating with tournament administration.

23.

Roster changes must follow the tournament's published roster policy.

24.

A player may not compete for two teams in the same match.

25.

Teams must verify their roster before the registration deadline.

26.

Unauthorized substitute players may not enter a competitive match.

27.

Team names must not contain prohibited, abusive or discriminatory content.

28.

Team logos and public branding must comply with tournament requirements.

29.

Teams are responsible for ensuring their players receive tournament announcements.

30.

Intentional roster manipulation to gain an unfair advantage is prohibited.

04

MATCH PROCEDURE

31.

Players must join the assigned lobby before the announced deadline.

32.

Players must use the correct tournament room or lobby information.

33.

Players must check the published match time in advance.

34.

Late arrival may be penalized according to the tournament schedule.

35.

Players must remain ready until the authorized match start.

36.

A match may only be restarted when tournament administration determines that a restart is justified.

37.

Players must not intentionally leave a match to create an unfair advantage.

38.

Players must follow the announced game mode and match format.

39.

Only the official tournament lobby settings may be used.

40.

Players must preserve relevant match evidence when a dispute occurs.

05

FAIR PLAY

41.

Cheating in any form is strictly prohibited.

42.

Unauthorized third-party software must not be used to gain a competitive advantage.

43.

Scripts, modified clients and unauthorized game modifications are prohibited.

44.

Exploiting unintended game behaviour for an unfair advantage is prohibited.

45.

Players must not intentionally abuse known glitches or exploits.

46.

Automation that gives a player an unfair competitive advantage is prohibited.

47.

Players must not interfere with the integrity of the game client.

48.

Collusion between opponents is prohibited unless specifically permitted by the tournament format.

49.

Match fixing, result manipulation and intentional throwing are prohibited.

50.

Any proven unfair competitive practice may result in penalties or disqualification.

06

DEVICES & SOFTWARE

51.

Players must compete using an approved device type specified by the tournament.

52.

Players must use an authorized and properly updated game client.

53.

Unauthorized overlays or competitive assistance tools are prohibited.

54.

External tools must not provide information unavailable through normal gameplay.

55.

Players are responsible for testing their device before competition.

56.

Battery, storage, connectivity and device performance are the player's responsibility unless the organizer states otherwise.

57.

Players must not use a device belonging to another competitor when prohibited by tournament policy.

58.

Unauthorized emulators or platforms are prohibited when the tournament requires mobile play.

59.

Device configuration must not be used to create an unintended competitive advantage.

60.

Tournament staff may investigate suspicious technical activity when evidence is available.

07

COMMUNICATION

61.

Teams must monitor the official communication channel throughout the tournament.

62.

Captains are responsible for receiving important administrative notices.

63.

Players must not impersonate tournament staff.

64.

Fake announcements, fake room codes and misleading information are prohibited.

65.

Harassment through tournament communication channels is prohibited.

66.

Threats against opponents, staff or community members are prohibited.

67.

Disagreements must be communicated through the official dispute process.

68.

Spam that interferes with tournament communication is prohibited.

69.

Players must not deliberately spread false information about results.

70.

Official tournament announcements take precedence over unofficial community messages.

08

RESULTS & EVIDENCE

71.

Match results must be recorded using the tournament's official result system.

72.

Players must not falsify screenshots or match evidence.

73.

Edited evidence presented as original evidence may result in disciplinary action.

74.

Teams should preserve screenshots or recordings when required by tournament instructions.

75.

Result disputes must be submitted through the official support process.

76.

Disputes should include the match number and relevant team information.

77.

Evidence should clearly show the information being disputed.

78.

False complaints may result in penalties.

79.

Administrators may request additional evidence when necessary.

80.

Published final results may only be changed after an authorized review.

09

PENALTIES

81.

Penalties must be proportionate to the violation and tournament circumstances.

82.

A warning may be issued for minor first-time violations where appropriate.

83.

Repeated violations may result in stronger penalties.

84.

Match points may be removed when a published rule allows such a penalty.

85.

A team may be disqualified for serious competitive violations.

86.

Individual players may be suspended where their conduct warrants it.

87.

Cheating-related penalties may apply to the player and, where justified, the team.

88.

Penalty decisions must be documented by tournament administration.

89.

Participants must comply with an announced penalty while an official review is pending unless otherwise directed.

90.

Battle Arena may update penalty guidelines when tournament conditions require clarification.

10

ADMINISTRATION & FINAL DECISIONS

91.

Tournament administrators are responsible for enforcing the published competition rules.

92.

Administrators may pause a competition when a serious technical or competitive issue occurs.

93.

Administrators may request information needed to investigate a reported violation.

94.

Participants must cooperate with legitimate tournament investigations.

95.

Organizers must publish important rule changes before they take effect whenever reasonably possible.

96.

Tournament schedules must be communicated through official channels.

97.

Administrators may correct an obvious scoring or publishing error after review.

98.

Participants may submit appeals or disputes through the tournament's designated process.

99.

The final outcome of an official dispute is determined by the authorized Battle Arena tournament administration under the applicable published rules.

100.

Every participant is expected to protect the integrity of competition: play fairly, respect others and compete with purpose.

BATTLE ARENA ESPORTS FEDERATION

PLAY FAIR. COMPETE HARD. LEAVE A LEGACY.

These rules exist to protect competitive integrity and give every player a fair opportunity to compete.
